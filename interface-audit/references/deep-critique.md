# Deep Critique

Use this reference only at `high` depth. It adds stronger framing, completeness, counteranalysis, and synthesis; it does not authorize more output or any writeback.

## Situate before judging

- Classify the artifact stage as concept, composition, polish, or production-equivalent from its real evidence. Judge concept work on structure and model, not missing polish.
- Make the brief explicit: surface job, audience, platform, stakes, constraints, focal concern, and connected screens.
- Observe before applying principles. Record what attracts attention, what action appears available, what the product communicates, and what contradicts the expected task.

## Separate analytical axes

- **Dimension:** structural, behavioral, or visual. When multiple apply, use the highest-level root cause.
- **Impact severity:** `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, or `LOW` from the main skill.
- **Confidence:** `Confirmed`, `Likely`, or `Judgment call` from the main skill.
- **Reach:** single state, repeated pattern, shared component, or system-wide rule.

Do not use severity as a proxy for confidence. A severe hypothesis can remain unverified; a minor defect can be certain.

## Completeness pass

A missing state or affordance is a grounded gap only when all three gates pass:

1. Name the trigger: a wait, commitment, handoff, interruption, reversible action, or unhappy path already present in the product model.
2. Search the inspected scope and adjacent states to confirm it is actually absent or explicitly deferred.
3. Cite the forcing element in the screen, flow, or brief that requires it.

Drop suggestions that merely sound useful. Keep at most five gaps, separate from the finding cap.

## Counteranalysis

For each candidate ask whether a competent product designer could reasonably defend the current choice because of stage, platform convention, user expertise, density, brand, experiment scope, technical constraint, or adjacent context. Re-read the evidence with that defense in mind.

- Drop candidates defeated by counterevidence.
- Mark tradeoff-dependent candidates `Judgment call` and state the deciding assumption.
- Do not backfill after dropping weak findings.
- Merge candidates with the same root cause before ranking.

## Justification rules

Ground the rationale in observed evidence, user task, product mechanics, and the principles bundled with this skill. This adaptation does not ship the upstream 606-entry UX knowledge base, facet ranker, or `UX-NNN` citation system: never invent those IDs or claim that the full KB was searched. Do not manufacture academic explanations or competitor comparisons. If external theory, a standard, or a current platform rule is essential, verify it independently and cite the authoritative source.

Finish with the dominant product risk, the highest-leverage recommendation, and the verification that would most change the verdict.
